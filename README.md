# Farmer-App
This is a decision‑support tool that uses NASA Earth observations along with local soil information, crop characteristics, and farmer priorities to help farmers explore rotation strategies that could strengthen soil health and adapt their farms to changing conditions.

## What We Intend To Do
Standard farming guides often assume textbook rainfall and neutral soils, leaving farmers vulnerable to shifting climate realities. Our objective is to bridge the gap between complex satellite telemetry and actionable, on-the-ground farming decisions by providing a tool that supports—but does not replace—local agricultural expertise.

Our application achieves this through the following core features:

* **Live Satellite Data Integration:** We query live NASA POWER climatology and SMAP root-zone soil moisture data for any coordinate on Earth to assess current rainfall anomalies, heat stress, and soil conditions.

* **Farmer-Centered Goal Selection:** Farmers can input their specific priorities—such as conserving water, healing tired soil, or maximizing short-term harvest yields—to customize the algorithm's recommendations.

* **Adaptive 3-Season Crop Rotations:** The tool compares multiple rotation strategies and generates a custom 3-season plan, explicitly explaining *why* each crop was suggested (e.g., nitrogen-fixing, deep-rooting) and how it benefits the soil.

* **Future Climate Horizon Modeling:** Beyond current conditions, the tool projects 1-year and 5-year (2030) warming and drying trends so farmers can proactively future-proof their fields against widening dry spells.

* **Plain-Language Diagnostics & Checklists:** We translate raw satellite telemetry into easily interpretable field status cards, interactive checklists, and clear risk warnings about what crops to avoid planting right now.
